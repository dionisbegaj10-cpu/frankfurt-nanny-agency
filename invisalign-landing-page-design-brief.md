# Design Brief: Premium Invisalign / Cosmetic Dentist Landing Page

## Creative Direction
Design a premium, ultra-modern landing page for a cosmetic dentist specializing in Invisalign treatment. The design should feel like a mix of Apple, Nothing, Tesla, and Linear.app — minimal, luxurious, immersive, and highly interactive.

The website should NOT look like a traditional dental website. It should feel like a premium product launch.

## Overall Style
- White, soft gray, and subtle glassmorphism
- Minimalistic luxury
- Lots of whitespace
- Rounded corners
- Smooth gradients
- Soft realistic shadows
- Premium typography
- Cinematic lighting
- Micro interactions everywhere
- Modern 3D objects
- Scroll-driven animations
- Smooth easing
- High-end product presentation

The website should feel expensive and futuristic.

## Hero Section
Full screen. Center stage is a photorealistic 3D Invisalign box floating in space, slowly rotating with realistic reflections.

Sequence on load:
1. The Invisalign box gently floats.
2. After 2 seconds, the lid slowly lifts by itself.
3. Inside is a glowing clear aligner.
4. The aligner slowly rises out of the box, rotating in mid-air.
5. Light refracts through the transparent plastic.
6. Small particles float around it; soft volumetric lighting shines from inside the box.
7. The aligner slowly spins while subtly following the mouse.

On scroll: the aligner leaves the box completely, becomes the main focus, and the camera follows it — cinematic, like an Apple product reveal.

**Headline:** "Your Future Smile Starts Here"
**Subheadline:** "Transform your smile with virtually invisible clear aligners designed for comfort, confidence, and precision."
**Primary CTA:** Book Your Consultation
**Secondary CTA:** See How Invisalign Works

## Scroll Animation
- Aligner stays floating, slowly rotating.
- Different camera angles reveal it; camera zooms closer.
- Transparent material catches light realistically.
- Background elements move with parallax.
- Entire experience feels like a premium product showcase.

## Section 2 — How Invisalign Works
Floating timeline instead of boring icons:
1. **Scan** — a 3D scanner appears; blue laser scan animation.
2. **Planning** — a transparent digital tooth model forms; wireframe transitions into realistic teeth.
3. **Treatment** — the aligner gently slides over the teeth.
4. **Smile** — teeth become perfectly aligned; beautiful white smile reveal.

Every transition animated.

## Section 3 — Interactive Smile Transformation
Split screen: before smile (left) / after smile (right). Draggable slider reveals the transformation. While dragging, individual teeth smoothly animate into alignment (real movement, not just image switching).

## Section 4 — Benefits
Cards floating in 3D, gently tilting with mouse movement. Icons are glass objects. Cards animate into place while scrolling.

- Nearly Invisible
- Comfortable Fit
- Removable
- Eat Anything
- Easy Cleaning
- Custom Treatment

## Section 5 — 3D Smile Simulation
Realistic floating jaw model. User can rotate and zoom. View buttons: Front, Side, Top, Smile View, Night View. Aligner snaps perfectly onto the teeth.

## Section 6 — Why Choose Our Clinic
Large premium cards, minimal icons, luxury photography, trust signals:
- Years of experience
- Certified Invisalign Provider
- Thousands of smiles transformed
- Latest technology
- 5-star reviews

## Section 7 — Testimonials
Floating glass cards that slowly move. Each contains: patient photo, quote, star rating, animated before/after smile.

## Section 8 — FAQ
Beautiful expanding glass accordion with smooth open/close animations.

## Final CTA
Massive hero-style ending. The aligner returns; the Invisalign box closes elegantly.
**Headline:** "Ready for Your Best Smile?"
Large premium booking button.

## Footer
Minimal, lots of whitespace, simple navigation, social icons, clinic information.

## Animation Style
Every animation should feel like Apple — nothing abrupt. Use:
- Spring animations
- Smooth easing
- Soft fade
- Parallax
- Depth
- Glass reflections
- Subtle motion blur
- Floating particles
- Volumetric lighting
- Realistic shadows
- Dynamic highlights
- Camera movement

The page should never feel static — everything should have subtle motion.

## 3D Style
The Invisalign aligner must look photorealistic:
- Transparent material
- Subsurface reflections
- Soft refraction
- Realistic highlights
- Tiny imperfections
- Studio lighting
- Ray-traced quality
- Product photography quality

## Technical Requirements
Build with:
- React
- Next.js
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion
- Three.js
- React Three Fiber
- Drei
- GSAP ScrollTrigger
- Lenis smooth scrolling

Use GPU-accelerated animations, optimize for desktop and mobile. All animations should degrade gracefully on lower-powered devices while preserving the premium experience.

## Summary
The final result should feel like a luxury product experience rather than a healthcare website — immersive, elegant, interactive, and memorable, with the Invisalign aligner as the centerpiece of the entire journey.
